#  AquaMind AI Drought Survival System
> **Think Water. Save Crops.**
AquaMind is an AI-powered drought survival system designed to help farmers manage limited water resources during prolonged drought conditions. It predicts crop stress, estimates water requirements, intelligently allocates available water, integrates alternative water sources, and enables precision root-level irrigation to maximize crop survival.
> 
## Problem
Prolonged drought creates severe water shortages for agriculture. When water is limited, providing equal irrigation to every crop zone is inefficient because different crops experience different levels of stress and have different water requirements.

Farmers need a system that can answer:

- Which crop zone is at the highest risk?
- How much water does each zone actually require?
- Which zones should receive water first?
- How can limited water be distributed to maximize crop survival?
- Can additional water be recovered from rainfall and atmospheric moisture?

##  Our Solution

**AquaMind** combines AI, IoT, precision irrigation, and water recovery into a single drought-management system.

The system continuously analyzes field conditions and:

1. Collects real-time farm and environmental data.
2. Predicts crop stress for each zone.
3. Estimates time-to-critical condition.
4. Calculates zone-wise water requirements.
5. Optimizes limited water allocation based on crop survival.
6. Integrates existing, rainwater, and recovered atmospheric water.
7. Delivers allocated water through root-level irrigation.
8. Continuously monitors conditions and re-optimizes the allocation.

##  Key Features

###  AI Crop Stress Prediction
Predicts crop stress levels using soil, weather, crop, and historical data.

###  Intelligent Water Allocation
Optimizes limited water resources by prioritizing crop zones where water provides the highest survival benefit.

###  Time-to-Critical Prediction
Estimates how close each crop zone is to critical water stress.

###  Atmospheric Water Recovery
Uses solid sorbents, solar regeneration, and condensation to recover usable water from atmospheric moisture.

###  Rainwater Integration
Captures and integrates available rainwater into the farm's usable water pool.

###  Precision Root-Level Irrigation
Delivers the allocated quantity directly to crop root zones using pumps, flow sensors, and solenoid valves.

###  Continuous Re-Optimization
Uses updated sensor data and crop conditions to continuously revise the water allocation plan.
